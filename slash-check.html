Odd/Even Slash Check

Append /xy to a digit string: x = last digit of the sum of odd positions, y = last digit of the sum of even positions.

Enter digits to compute a checksum, or digits/xy to validate one.

How it works

Positions are counted from the left starting at 1. The 1st, 3rd, 5th… digits are summed and the ones place becomes x; the 2nd, 4th, 6th… digits are summed and the ones place becomes y. The result is written as digits/xy.

This catches any single-digit error and any swap of two adjacent digits (they land in different parity classes). It does not catch swapping two digits that are both in odd positions or both in even positions, nor an error of exactly 10 in one digit.
