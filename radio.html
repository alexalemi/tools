This service only starts from inside its own player

A station is a list of links. It plays through them in order, or shuffled — a shuffled station plays every link once before any repeats. Links can be mixed: a YouTube video, a Bandcamp album and an Icecast stream can share one station, and each link is played by whichever engine suits it. Type is read from the link, so there is nothing to set. Stream and audio links play directly with full control. YouTube and SoundCloud run through their player APIs, so they advance on their own. Spotify and Bandcamp only start from a click inside their own embed, and cannot auto-advance. Bandcamp needs its embed code, not the album address: open the album, choose Share › Embed, and paste what it gives you — the whole <iframe> snippet is fine. A plain bandcamp.com album link cannot be played, because the player is keyed by an id that only exists inside that page. Stream links must be https:// — browsers block plain http audio on a secure page. Stations live in this browser's local storage; download a backup before clearing site data. Arrow keys change station, space plays and pauses, N skips a track, S toggles shuffle.
