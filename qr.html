Tips

Higher error correction lets the code survive smudges and small logos, but makes it denser. For long text, drop to L. Keep a light background and dark foreground; scanners expect dark modules on light.

Wi-Fi codes use the WIFI:T:WPA;S:name;P:password;; format that iOS and Android cameras recognize. Contacts are encoded as a vCard.
